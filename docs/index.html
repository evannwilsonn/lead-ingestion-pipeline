Lead Pipeline

Lead Pipeline turns website inquiry emails into organized, prioritized leads. It reads new inquiry emails in a connected Gmail inbox, extracts the contact details and request, sorts each lead into a priority tier, and records it in the business's own Airtable base so the team can respond faster.

How it works

  • Checks the connected Gmail inbox for unread emails with a specific subject line, such as "New Website Inquiry".
  • Extracts the sender's name, email, phone number, budget and a short summary of the request.
  • Saves the lead to the business's Airtable base, and can optionally text the team about high-priority leads.
  • Labels each processed email in Gmail so nothing is handled twice.

Watch the one-minute walkthrough of four real test inquiries, or see the source code.

Lead Pipeline only acts on matching inquiry emails. It does not send email from your account, and it does not read or store other messages.
